## Comment les utiliser ?

- 1re colonne : ==base verbale== (infinitif sans *to*) → présent, futur, après un modal.
- 2e colonne : ==prétérit== → past simple (*I **went***).
- 3e colonne : ==participe passé== → present perfect (*I have **gone***) et voix passive (*It was **built***).

> [!astuce] Les apprendre par familles
> - Les 3 formes identiques : cut-cut-cut, put-put-put, cost-cost-cost, set-set-set, read-read-read (mais prononcé « red » au passé).
> - Prétérit = participe : bring-brought-brought, buy-bought-bought, think-thought-thought, teach-taught-taught.
> - Le son « i / a / u » : begin-began-begun, drink-drank-drunk, run-ran-run, swim-swam-swum.

=== QUIZ ===
? Prétérit de « buy » ?
- buyed
+ bought
- brought
! buy – bought – bought. Attention : brought vient de bring.

? Participe passé de « write » ?
- wrote
+ written
- writed
! write – wrote – written.

? « I have ___ my password. » (forget)
- forgot
+ forgotten
- forget
! Après have : participe passé → forgotten.

? « Yesterday, the technician ___ a new switch. » (bring)
+ brought
- bringed
- brung
! Past simple de bring : brought.

? Lequel a ses 3 formes identiques ?
- send
+ put
- take
! put – put – put.

? « The network was ___ in 2021. » (build)
- builded
+ built
- build
! Voix passive : be + participe passé → built.
